https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/virginia-woolf/mrs-dalloway

- Lilac: the sentence beginning “Ah yes—so she breathed…” (flower-shop paragraph).
- Carnations, composed appearance: the sentence beginning “And then, opening her eyes…” (same paragraph).
- Carnations, Hugh's gift: the paragraph-long sentence beginning “A magnificent figure he cut too…”.
- Roses, Different Realities: the complete sentence supplied by the user also matches this edition.
- Roses, Unspoken Love: subsequently replaced with the user-provided short and expanded passages beginning “He was holding out flowers—roses, red and white roses.” Its two-paragraph context is also the user’s replacement text.

Whitespace is normalized and invisible word-joiner characters around em dashes
are removed. The missing University of Virginia PDF has not been checked;
this identifies the edition actually used rather than claiming PDF verification.
Contexts and reflection questions remain the user's editorial text.
